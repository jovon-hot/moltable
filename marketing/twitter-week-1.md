## Day 2 — 技术深度
AI agents have memory layers. But nobody built the identity layer — until now.

Here's the difference:
• Memory: remembers WHAT you do
• Identity: remembers WHO you are

Both are essential. One was missing.

## Day 3 — 对比 mem0
mem0 is brilliant at memory. 62K stars deserved.

But here's what it doesn't solve:
• Cross-agent identity
• Computer migration
• Persona switching

That's not a deficiency — it's a different layer. We built the identity one.
